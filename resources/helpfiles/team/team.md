# Team Panel Help

## Purpose

The Team panel is the operational ownership workspace for the five Team Operating System lifecycles: Workflow, Offering, Resource, Knowledge, and Supply. Use this panel to maintain reusable definitions and operational records owned by a team.

## Workflow

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Workflows** | Design and manage workflow definitions. |
| **Work Packages** | Define reusable ordered sets of work activities. |
| **Checklists** | Define reusable non-sequential checklist templates used by work packages or workflows. |
| **Forms** | Define forms used during workflow execution. |

## Offering

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Offerings** | Maintain services and offerings owned by the team. |
| **Catalog Items** | Maintain requestable catalog items presented to users. |

## Resource

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Assignments** | Manage workforce assignments and allocations. |
| **Events** | Review onboarding, transfers, promotions, offboarding, and other workforce changes. |
| **Roles** | Maintain team roles and responsibilities. |
| **Skills** | Maintain the organizational skills inventory. |
| **Competencies** | Maintain competency requirements and assessments. |

## Knowledge

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Documents** | Maintain team-owned documentation. |
| **Tutorials** | Maintain tutorials and learning guides. |
| **Templates** | Maintain reusable team templates and tools. |
| **Learning Modules** | Maintain formal learning content. |

## Supply

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Items** | Maintain items managed by the team. |
| **Inventory** | Monitor stock levels and inventory holdings. |
| **Suppliers** | Maintain supplier and vendor information. |
| **Movements** | Track inventory transactions and movements. |

## Ownership Guidance

- Use **Workflow** for how work is executed.
- Use **Offering** for what the team provides.
- Use **Resource** for who performs the work.
- Use **Knowledge** for information that enables the work.
- Use **Supply** for items and materials that support the work.
