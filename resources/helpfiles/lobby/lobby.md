# Lobby Panel Help

## Purpose

The Lobby panel provides essential information and actions for users before entering the main staff workspace. Use this panel to maintain personal details, complete induction activities, read policies, and access emergency guidance.

## Available Navigation

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | View and maintain personal information, photo, contact details, and preferences. |
| **Induction** | Browse onboarding and induction learning modules and complete assigned tests. |
| **Policies** | Browse company policies and governance documents. |
| **Emergency** | View the Emergency Response Escalation Matrix and identify the appropriate emergency contact or response path. |

## Getting Started

1. Open **Profile** and verify that personal and contact information is current.
2. Open **Induction** to complete assigned learning modules and tests.
3. Use **Policies** to read applicable company guidance.
4. Use **Emergency** when urgent response or escalation guidance is required.

> **Important:** For an immediate emergency, follow the site's established emergency response procedure and contact the designated emergency responder.
