# Staff Panel Help

## Purpose

The Staff panel is the daily employee workspace. Use it to complete work, manage accountable items, request services, access knowledge and learning, raise issues, find organizational resources, and open approved systems.

## Task

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Assigned** | View and complete assigned workflow tasks and pending certification actions. |
| **Upcoming** | View certifications that are nearing expiry. |
| **Opened** | View and pick open tasks available for assignment. |
| **Read** | View unread and latest memos. |

## Accountable

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | View and maintain personal, staff, and job-position information, including photo and contact details. |
| **Equipment** | View equipment, devices, and tools under personal accountability. |
| **Storage** | View allocated personal spaces such as cubicles, lockers, shoe lockers, drawers, or other storage locations. |

## Catalog

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Menu** | Browse available services and offerings. |
| **Request for Something** | Start a request and track or monitor its progress. |

## Knowledge

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Documents** | Access company knowledge and reference documents. |
| **Tools** | Access reusable templates, tools, techniques, and forms that can be populated for a specific purpose. |

## Learning

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Courses** | Participate in training programmes. |
| **Modules** | Complete learning modules and tutorials. |
| **Quizzes** | Complete learning assessments. |
| **Certifications** | Track certification requirements, progress, and status. |

## Escalation

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Report an Issue** | Raise an incident ticket for equipment, facility, IT, or other fault reporting. |
| **Contact Someone** | Use the Sensitive Matter Escalation Matrix to identify an appropriate contact. |
| **Emergency** | Use the Emergency Response Escalation Matrix to identify the appropriate emergency response path. |

## Resources

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Staff** | Search and browse employee directory information. |
| **Teams** | Browse team information and membership. |
| **Organization Units** | Browse the organization structure. |
| **Locations** | Browse sites, buildings, and work locations. |

## Systems

| Navigation Item | What You Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Systems** | Access approved business systems and applications. |

## Recommended Daily Flow

1. Check **Assigned**, **Upcoming**, **Opened**, and **Read**.
2. Review accountable equipment or storage when needed.
3. Use **Catalog** to request something.
4. Use **Knowledge**, **Learning**, or **Resources** to find supporting information.
5. Use **Escalation** for incident reporting, sensitive matters, or emergency guidance.
